# meetings

The meetings of the board: the list by period, and the detail with its agenda, resolutions, participants and documents.

| Layer             | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | May import                                                      |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `domain/`         | `entities/` (`Meeting`, `MeetingSummary`, `AgendaItem`, `Resolution`, `Participant`, `Document`), `value-objects/` (`MeetingPeriod`, `VoteChoice`), `policies/votingState.ts`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | `core/domain`, `auth/domain`, its own files, no package         |
| `application/`    | `ports/` (`MeetingRepository`, `MeetingCache`, `MeetingDocumentEvictor`), `loadMeeting.ts` (the meeting through the offline policy), `MeetingCachePurger.ts` (the `SessionDataPurger` of `auth` for the cached meetings), `use-cases/` (`ListMeetings`, `GetMeeting`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `domain`, `core`, `auth`, its own files, no package             |
| `infrastructure/` | `HttpMeetingRepository` and its wire classes (`dto/meetingDto.ts`, which also describes a document as a meeting lists it), `persistence/` (`SqliteMeetingCache`, the stored form of a meeting `storedMeeting.ts`, and the tables it owns `schema.ts`), `InMemoryMeetingCache`                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | `domain`, `application`, `core`, the packages it adapts         |
| `presentation/`   | `screens/` (`MeetingsListScreen`, `MeetingDetailScreen`), `components/` (`MeetingRow`, `AgendaItemView`, `ResolutionView`, `ResolutionVoteLine`, `ParticipantRow`, `DocumentRow`, `badgeTones`), `hooks/` (`useMeetingsList`, `meetingPages`, `useMeeting`), `slots/resolutionActions.tsx` (what a feature adds under a resolution), `state/meetingsStore.tsx`, `services/MeetingsServices.tsx`, `query/meetingsQueryKeys.ts`, `i18n/meetingMessageKeys.ts`, `navigation/useOpenMeeting.ts`, `testIds.ts` | `domain`, `application`, `core`, `auth`, never `infrastructure` |

- `domain` and `application` are pure: they run in plain Node, with no package.
- The `Document` entity is here, as the meeting holds its documents. Opening one is the feature `documents`; the wire form of a listed document is here too (`meetingDto.ts`), so that the HTTP adapter of a meeting does not import the one of a document.
- The in-memory repository of the development build is in `src/composition/dev/in-memory`: it reads the simulated server.
- `meetings` depends on `core` and `auth`, and on nothing else. Three things that look like dependencies on other features are inverted: `loadMeeting` drops the documents of a refused meeting through the `MeetingDocumentEvictor` port (`documents` implements it with `DocumentCacheEvictor`), the cache of meetings is emptied at the end of a session through `MeetingCachePurger` (a `SessionDataPurger` of `auth`), and the detail screen shows what a feature adds under a resolution through the `ResolutionActionsSlot` (`votes` implements it with `VOTE_RESOLUTION_ACTIONS`, and the composition plugs it). Without the slot a resolution still tells the vote the server recorded (`ResolutionVoteLine`) and offers nothing to do.
- `withVoteRecorded` (a meeting with a vote recorded on its resolution) is not here: it is in `votes/domain`, and `src/composition/glue/MeetingCacheVoteRecorder.ts` applies it to the cached meeting.
- `SqliteMeetingCache` reads the names of its tables from `infrastructure/persistence/schema.ts`, the slice of the schema that `meetings` owns; `src/composition/persistence/schema.ts` assembles the slices and `migrations.ts` keeps the history of the file.
