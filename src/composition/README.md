# composition

The composition of the application: the one place that knows every feature and chooses an implementation for each port. It is the `lib/app` of the Flutter twin; it is not called `app` here because the root `app/` folder is the routes of expo-router (thin files that mount what the composition gives them).

| Folder         | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Follows the rules of                                |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `container/`   | The composition root: the two configurations (`mock`, `http`), the ports that each builds (`mockPorts`, `httpPorts`), `wireUseCases`, `createContainer`, the choice of the monitoring, the notices of the start. It imports no package                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | its own element, `container`                        |
| `persistence/` | The database of the whole file: `schema.ts` (the schema of the app, assembled from the slices that each feature owns in its `infrastructure/persistence/schema.ts`) and `migrations.ts` (the history of the file, which spans the features and whose SQL never changes). The opening machinery is in `src/core/infrastructure/persistence`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `infrastructure`                                    |
| `device/`      | `DevicePlatform`, the seams the adapters are written against (database engine, secure storage, random bytes, biometric prompt, network state), and `createExpoPlatform`, the one of a phone. It bundles adapters of the core and of `auth`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `infrastructure`                                    |
| `dev/`         | The simulated world of the mock configuration, which spans every feature (below)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | `infrastructure`                                    |
| `shell/`       | The app shell: `navigation/` (the navigator and its protected screens, the error boundary, the system links), `services/` (`AppServices`, `AppProviders`, which assembles the contexts of the features, the slots and the query client, and `StartupGate`), `session/` (the controller of the session and of the life of the app, the lock on the way back, the privacy cover, the connection), `errorCatalog.ts` (the text of every error code, put together from the keys of the features), `testIds.ts` (the ids of the shell), `components/` (the header actions), `screens/` (the start failure) | `presentation`                                      |
| `glue/`        | What links features that may not import each other, and the tests that need every feature at once: `sessionDataPurgers` (the list of purgers `auth` is given), `MeetingCacheVoteRecorder` (the `RecordedVoteSink` of `votes` over the `MeetingCache` of `meetings`), `retryableCodes` (the codes of the core and of each feature, joined), and the tests of the values that are stored, the limits and the security scenarios                                                                                                                                                                         | `application` (they run in the Jest `core` project) |

`dev/` is the simulated world of the development build and of the tests:

| Folder       | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `in-memory/` | The simulated server (`InMemoryBackend`, `SimulatedNetwork`) and its seed, made of the fixtures (`fixtures/`: the loader that shifts the dates by whole days, the bundled files), and the adapters of the mock configuration that read the simulated server: the session, the biometric gate, the connectivity that follows the simulated network, and the three repositories. The caches, the outbox and the screen protection are in their feature |
| `mock-api/`  | The routes of `docs/api.md` over the simulated server (`MockApi`): the real HTTP adapters run unchanged against it in the tests, and `npm run mock:api` serves it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `pdf/`       | A small PDF writer for the demo documents                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

- It may import anything of `src/core` and `src/features`, and the packages behind the adapters, within the rules of the layer each folder follows (a folder of the shell cannot import an adapter, a folder of `dev` cannot import a screen).
- Nothing in `src/core` or `src/features` imports it: that is a lint error, as is a feature importing the infrastructure of another. Where a feature needs something of another that it may not import, it declares a port, a slot or a context, another feature implements it, and this folder wires them: `AuthSessionGate` for the `SessionGate` of the core, `MeetingCachePurger` and `DocumentCachePurger` for the `SessionDataPurger` of `auth`, `DocumentCacheEvictor` for the `MeetingDocumentEvictor` of `meetings`, `MeetingCacheVoteRecorder` for the `RecordedVoteSink` of `votes`, `VOTE_RESOLUTION_ACTIONS` for the slot of the resolutions of `meetings`, the error catalog and the retryable codes for the core.
- The features depend on each other in one direction only, and the graph is a named constant checked by the linter (`FEATURE_GRAPH` in `tooling/featureGraph.mjs`): every feature on `core`, `meetings` on `auth`, `documents` and `votes` on `auth` and `meetings`, and the composition on all of them. [`docs/architecture.md`](../../docs/architecture.md) tells the layout and the rules.
- Every user-facing text comes from the translation resources (`src/core/presentation/i18n/resources`), never from a literal.
